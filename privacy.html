সেনানথি (Shenanothi)Your service file, on your phone

Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Summary

সেনানথি stores your documents only in your own Google Drive. The developer has no server and cannot see, collect or share your data.

What the app accesses

  • Your Google account email and name, to show who is logged in.
  • One hidden app folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). The app can read and write only the files it creates there. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

What is stored, and where

  • The document register, your BAFZ 2043 data, your photo and the scans you upload are saved in that hidden folder in your own Google Drive.
  • Your browser may keep small settings on your device, such as day or night mode and your last used email address.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer or to any other server.

Encryption

If you choose a passphrase when you start, everything is encrypted on your device (AES-256) before it is uploaded. The passphrase is never stored or sent anywhere. If you forget it, you can set a new one by signing in again with the same Gmail account, so anyone with access to that Gmail account could do the same. Keep your Gmail secure and turn on Google's 2-Step Verification.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. Your use of Google services is also covered by Google's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

  • Delete any document inside the app, and its scan is removed from your Drive.
  • To remove everything: open Google Account → Third-party apps and services, choose সেনানথি, and remove its access. Google then lets you delete the app's hidden data.
  • Export a backup from Settings first if you want to keep a copy.

Your responsibility

Upload only documents you are permitted to keep on a personal Google account. Do not upload classified documents, and follow your unit's IT and security instructions.
